Most of what gets written about palletizing is either a vendor brochure or a
spec sheet. Neither helps much when you're standing at the end of a line
trying to decide whether a pattern that fits two more cases per layer is
going to arrive at the DC in one piece.

The Stack Report is where we'll write about the part in between: the
engineering judgment calls that turn a case size and a pallet footprint into
a load that ships well.

## What you'll find here

- **Pattern fundamentals.** Ti-Hi, interlocking, column stacks versus block and pinwheel patterns, and when each one earns its place.
- **Case and corrugate decisions.** How small changes to case dimensions move the numbers on the pallet, and on the trailer.
- **Load stability.** Overhang, compression strength, and why the pattern with the most cases isn't always the right one.
- **Trailer and freight math.** Working backward from the truck to the pallet.
- **Product updates.** What's new in Betsel Stack and why we built it.

## The Industry Wire

Below our articles you'll find the **Industry Wire**, a running feed of
packaging, palletizing, and supply chain headlines from across the trade
press. It refreshes itself every hour, and every headline links straight to
the original publisher.
